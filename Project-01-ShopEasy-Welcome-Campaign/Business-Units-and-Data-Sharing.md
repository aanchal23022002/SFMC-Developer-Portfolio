# Business Units and Data Sharing

## Project
ShopEasy Welcome Campaign

## Business Scenario

ShopEasy Group operates three brands:

- ShopEasy Fashion
- ShopEasy Electronics
- ShopEasy Grocery

Each brand has separate marketing operations.

## Proposed Business Unit Structure

Parent Business Unit:
ShopEasy Global

Child Business Units:
1. ShopEasy Fashion
2. ShopEasy Electronics
3. ShopEasy Grocery

## Why Use Multiple Business Units?

- Organize brand-specific campaigns
- Manage user access
- Support independent marketing operations
- Enable controlled sharing of resources

## User Roles

Marketing Specialist:
Creates and manages marketing content.

SFMC Developer:
Develops queries, automations, and journeys.

Administrator:
Manages account configuration and permissions.

These are illustrative responsibilities.
Actual access depends on assigned permissions.

## Shared Data Extensions

A Shared Data Extension can provide
authorized Business Units access to
common structured data.

Example:
Global_Product_Master

## Shared Content

Approved templates and content assets
can be shared where supported and authorized.

## Security Considerations

- Follow least-privilege access
- Respect customer consent
- Avoid unnecessary sharing
- Use fictional data for practice

## Implementation Status

Conceptual design and learning documentation.

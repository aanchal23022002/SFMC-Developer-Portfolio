# Welcome Journey Design

## Journey Name

ShopEasy New Customer Welcome Journey

## Entry Source

Newly registered eligible customers.

## Journey Steps

Step 1:
Customer registers on the website.

Step 2:
Customer information is stored.

Step 3:
Check registration date and marketing consent.

Step 4:
Eligible customer enters the welcome journey.

Step 5:
Send personalized welcome email.

Step 6:
Monitor campaign results.

## Exclusion Conditions

Exclude customers who:

- Have not provided marketing consent.
- Have unsubscribed.
- Have already received the welcome email.

- Data Extensions
- Content Builder
- Journey Builder

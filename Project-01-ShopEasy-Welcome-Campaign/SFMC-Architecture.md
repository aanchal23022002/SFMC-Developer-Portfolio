# Salesforce Marketing Cloud Architecture

## Project

ShopEasy Welcome Campaign

## Company Overview

ShopEasy Group is a fictional e-commerce company
operating three brands:

1. ShopEasy Fashion
2. ShopEasy Electronics
3. ShopEasy Grocery

## Proposed Account Structure

Parent Business Unit:
ShopEasy Group

Child Business Units:
- ShopEasy Fashion
- ShopEasy Electronics
- ShopEasy Grocery

## Business Requirements

Each brand should manage its own marketing
campaigns and customer journeys.

Access to marketing data and assets should
be controlled according to business needs.

## SFMC Components

Contact Builder:
Organizes customer information and relationships.

Content Builder:
Creates and manages marketing content.

Journey Builder:
Automates customer engagement journeys.

Automation Studio:
Automates data processing activities.

## Learning Outcomes

- Understood Enterprise account architecture.
- Understood Parent and Child Business Units.
- Learned the purpose of MIDs.
- Explored SFMC navigation.

## Implementation Status

Conceptual architecture design.

No Business Units were created or modified.

# Email Studio Configuration

## Project
ShopEasy Welcome Campaign

## Campaign Objective
Send welcome emails to newly registered
customers who have provided marketing consent.

## Audience Eligibility
- Newly registered customers
- Valid email address
- Required marketing consent
- Not unsubscribed
- Not otherwise suppressed

## Proposed Sender Profile
From Name: ShopEasy
From Email: welcome@shopeasy.example

## Proposed Send Classification
Commercial

## Proposed Suppression Rules
- Customers without marketing consent
- Unsubscribed customers
- Customers already sent the welcome email
- Invalid email addresses

## Tracking Metrics
- Sent
- Delivered
- Bounced
- Opened
- Clicked
- Unsubscribed

## Implementation Status
Conceptual configuration only.

No live emails have been sent.

# Project 01: ShopEasy Welcome Campaign

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates the conceptual design
of a customer welcome email campaign using
Salesforce Marketing Cloud Engagement.

## Business Scenario

ShopEasy is a fictional e-commerce company.

The company wants to automatically send welcome
emails to newly registered customers who have
provided marketing consent.

## Business Requirements

1. Store customer information.
2. Identify newly registered customers.
3. Check marketing consent.
4. Send welcome emails to eligible customers.
5. Monitor email campaign performance.

## Salesforce Marketing Cloud Components

- Data Extensions
- Contact Builder
- Content Builder
- Journey Builder
- Automation Studio

## Learning Objectives

- Understand Salesforce Marketing Cloud.
- Understand customer segmentation.
- Learn how customer journeys work.
- Understand marketing automation.

## Project Status

Conceptual design completed.

Hands-on SFMC implementation is pending.
